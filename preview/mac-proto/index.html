MAC · Multi-Ad Chaining

Interactive prototype · a grid of ads; Oakley and Starbucks each seed their own Reels ad chain · the FMSS IAB sheet and edge swipe back, shared

A Suggested for you grid of ads. Tap the Oakley ad: it grows into Reels and seeds a chain of related ads; swipe up through it (Oakley → LensCrafters → Ray-Ban). The Starbucks ad seeds its own chain the same way (Starbucks → Blue Bottle → La Colombe). Learn more on the Oakley ad opens oakley.com, and on the Ray-Ban ad ray-ban.com, in the in-app browser full screen; × or Escape returns to the reel. Back (or an edge swipe from the left) returns to the grid, into the ad that seeded the chain.
