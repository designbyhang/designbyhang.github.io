Case Study · Citi

Citi Mobile

Defining a modular mobile design language for Citi's 18 million users.

Role
Lead Product Designer
Scope
Design System + Product Innovation
Timeline
2017–2018

01 — Overview

In 2017, I was brought in to define Citi's mobile visual language, establishing a modular tile architecture and color theming across three customer segments. I led the development of its first mobile design system, scaling it across the FinTech and Core design teams.

Working closely with Product and Research, we co-created new banking experiences with customers, bringing features including Account Aggregation and Spending Insights from testing through launch, reaching 18M+ active mobile users.
