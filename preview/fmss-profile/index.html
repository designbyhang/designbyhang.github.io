Tap Nike on the reel to open its profile. A peek of nike.com slides up; scroll the profile and the peek collapses into a floating card (scrolling back up keeps the card until you reach the very top, where the peek springs back). Tap the peek or the card to expand the full-screen in-app browser (IAB); × or Escape closes it back to the card, and a swipe down (from the header, or a pull past the page top) morphs it into the floating card. On the reel, Learn more opens the same IAB as Caption & Browse: drag it down to a half sheet or away. The card’s × dismisses only the card. Back, or a swipe from the left edge, returns to the reel.
