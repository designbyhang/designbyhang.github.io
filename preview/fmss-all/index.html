Interactive prototype · Solution 1 and Solution 2, one phone, a Menu to switch

FM Split Screen

Tap Menu (top left, where the time sits) to pick any of the FMSS Solution 1 and Solution 2 experiences; swipe the menu's pages or tap its tabs. In each: tap the ad (its media or CTA; swipe up on the Story) to open split screen. In split screen drag the scrubber or tap the video to pause; tap or swipe up the sheet for full screen; swipe it, or the video, down to go back; × closes.
