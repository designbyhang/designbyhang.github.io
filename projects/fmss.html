Case Study · Facebook · FMSS

Facebook Monetization
Split Screen

Building a shared interaction model for Facebook’s ad experiences.

Role
Design Owner
Platforms
iOS, Android, iPadOS
Timeline
2023-2025

01 — Overview

In 2023, I developed FMSS, a design framework to unify Facebook’s ad experiences. I aligned stakeholders across Meta organizations and scaled its adoption throughout the app, establishing it as a canonical framework for Facebook Design.

Its interaction patterns went on to influence broader industry approaches to ad engagement. In 2025, FMSS generated over $51M in incremental revenue for Meta.

02 — Problem

A fragmented experience, multiplied by a fragmented organization

Facebook had accumulated years of localized product decisions, leaving people with inconsistent navigation patterns across surfaces.

Ad experiences were no exception. Teams across Meta's monetization efforts independently built split screen experiences for different ad objectives, each optimized for their immediate needs.

Without a unified framework in place, every new ad objective introduced a new way of interacting with split screen. The ad experience became harder to predict, and consistency became even harder to achieve.

03 — Key Insights

Different objectives,
same user task

Ads served a range of objectives, from Conversions and Lead Generation to Business Message and App Installs, but they shared an underlying task: moving people from discovery to engagement.

Disconnected teams,
duplicated effort

Without a unified strategy or shared guidance, teams had to reinvent solutions independently, increasing development costs and making it harder to evolve the ad experience as a whole.

04 — Opportunity

How might we unify fragmented ad experiences
to create greater consistency and make them easier
to evolve?

  1. Establish a shared interaction modelTo create familiarity and reinforce a consistent mental model across ad experiences.
  2. Build reusable infrastructureTo enable system-level updates, reduce maintenance costs, and accelerate new monetization opportunities.

05 — Solution

FMSS: A design framework that establishes a consistent interaction model across ad objectives and Facebook surfaces

06 — Validation & Adoption

Adoption is where a framework proves its value

Because ad experiences spanned multiple organizations beyond Facebook Monetization (Browser Ecosystem, App & Gaming, Business Messaging, and more), I worked team by team to galvanize cross-functional partners around a unified vision through design advocacy, strategic reviews, and close collaboration.

Through a series of cross-org experiments, we validated the framework across existing use cases, demonstrating its ability to improve user experience and business outcomes.

07 — System & Scale

A structured system,
built for growth

The unification effort went beyond fixing a fragmented user experience. It reshaped how video ad post clicks were architected by establishing a layered system that separated shared infrastructure from experience-specific logic. This scalable foundation enabled us to validate new features and iterate holistically across split screen experiences, from the way media and the bottom sheet divide the screen to how video controls, gestures, and transitions behave.

With the system in place, we brought ad split screen experiences to Facebook on iPad for the first time.

  • Split RatioMore flexible anchor points
  • Tap to Pause/ResumeMore intuitive video playback
  • Media ControlsSimplified UI with auto-hide
  • Entry/Exit AnimationsSmoother transitions between ad states
  • Gesture InteractionsRefined swipe and drag behaviors
  • Scrubber & ThumbnailFaster navigation with previews

08 — Evolution

New experiences, one cohesive system

FMSS continues to evolve, enabling new monetization opportunities across Facebook. Here are a few key projects powered by FMSS, each designed to help people explore ads in context while driving more qualified traffic for advertisers.

Caption & Browse

Gives people a quick preview of the ad’s destination the moment they want to learn more, without pulling them out of Reels.

H-Scroll & Browse

Turns multi-product ads into a swipe-able split screen, so people can browse product details side by side without leaving the ad.

Profile & Browse

Lets people peek at the ad’s destination without leaving the brand’s Profile.

09 — Results

Incremental Revenue generated for Meta in 2025 through feature, surface, and platform expansion enabled by FMSS.

6.4 EYS

Engineering Years Saved for Facebook through reduced operational costs in product development, testing, and SEV response, enabled by FMSS.
