Interactive prototype · Solution 1 and Solution 2, one phone, a Menu to switch

FM Split Screen

Tap Menu (top left, where the time sits) to pick any of the FMSS Solution 1 and Solution 2 experiences. In each: tap the ad's media (or swipe up on the Story) to open split screen; tap the Feed ad's footer / CTA to open the destination full screen. In split screen drag the scrubber or tap the video to pause; tap or swipe up the sheet for full screen; swipe it, or the video, down to go back; × closes to Feed.
