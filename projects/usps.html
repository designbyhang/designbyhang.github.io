01 — Overview

USPS was ready to unveil its latest innovations to America. What it didn’t need was another dense, overly educational product demo. With full creative control, I saw an opportunity to experiment, building a bold, colorful visual language through illustration and animation that turned dry product information into an experience that felt approachable, memorable, and fun to explore.
