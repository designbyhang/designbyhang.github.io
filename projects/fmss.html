Case study · Facebook · FMSS

Facebook Monetization Split Screen

Building a shared interaction model for Facebook’s ad experiences.

Role
Design STO
Platforms
iOS, Android, iPadOS
Timeline
2023-2025

01 — Overview

FMSS is a design framework I created in 2023 to bring consistency and cohesion to user experiences across Facebook ad products. I championed the vision across multiple Meta organizations, driving adoption across the app and establishing FMSS as a canonical framework for Facebook Design.

Its interaction patterns went on to influence broader industry approaches to ad engagement. In 2025, FMSS generated over $51M in incremental revenue for Meta.

02 — Problem

A fragmented experience, multiplied by a fragmented organization

Facebook had accumulated years of localized product decisions, leaving people with inconsistent navigation patterns across surfaces.

Ad experiences were no exception. Teams across Meta's monetization efforts independently built split screen experiences for different ad objectives, each optimized for their immediate needs.

Without a unified framework and strategy, every new ad objective introduced a new way of interacting with split screen. The ad experience became harder to predict, and consistency became even harder to achieve.

03 — Key Insights

Different objectives, same user task

Ads served a range of objectives, including Onsite and Offsite Conversions, Lead Generation, Business Messages, and App Installs, but they shared an underlying task: moving people from discovery to engagement.

Disconnected teams, duplicated effort

Without a unified strategy or shared guidance, teams had to reinvent solutions independently, increasing development costs and making it harder to evolve the ad experience as a whole.

04 — Opportunity

How might we unify fragmented ad experiences
to create greater consistency and make the system easier
to evolve?

  1. Establish a shared interaction modelTo create familiarity and reinforce a consistent mental model across ad experiences.
  2. Build reusable infrastructureTo enable system-level updates, reduce maintenance costs, and accelerate new monetization opportunities.

05 — Solution

FMSS:
A design framework that establishes a consistent interaction model across ad objectives and Facebook surfaces.

06 — Validation & Adoption

A framework only creates value when teams can adopt it and drive meaningful results.

Because ad experiences spanned multiple organizations beyond Facebook Monetization (Browser Ecosystem, App & Gaming, Business Messaging, and more), I galvanized teams around a unified vision through design advocacy, strategic reviews, and cross-functional collaboration.

Through a series of cross-org experiments, we validated the framework across existing use cases, demonstrating its ability to improve user experience and business outcomes.

07 — System & Scale

From a fix to an architecture

The unification effort went beyond fixing a fragmented user experience. It reshaped how video ad post clicks were architected by establishing a layered system that separated shared infrastructure from experience specific logic.

Layer 1Base Framework
  • Gesture Configurations
  • BtmSheet Container Anchors
  • Overall Stateful UI Adjustments: Drag Handle, Dismiss Btn, etc.
Layer 2Abstractions & APIs
  • Common UI Elements & Layouts
  • Top View Lifecycle Management
  • BtmSheet Lifecycle Management
  • BtmSheet State Handling, Transition & Logging
Layer 3Product Integration
  • Top View: Video, Image, Text, H-Scroll, etc.
  • BtmSheet: IAB, LG, CTM, MAI, Product Catalog, etc.

This scalable foundation enabled faster iteration and system-level improvements, while creating a path for future growth. With the system in place, we brought ad split screen experiences to Facebook on iPad for the first time.

08 — Evolution

New experiences, same foundation

FMSS continues to evolve, enabling new monetization opportunities across Facebook. Here are a few key projects powered by FMSS.

Caption & Browse

A Reels ad expands its caption, then the advertiser’s site rises as a bottom sheet and opens to full height.

H-Scroll Split Screen

A carousel ad opens into split screen: each item in the carousel updates the site below, until the browser expands to full height.

Profile & Browse

The same model extended to a Reels-style ad and its advertiser profile.
