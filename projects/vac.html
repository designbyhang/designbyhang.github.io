Case Study · Facebook · VAC

Video Ad Chaining

Paving a new path to ad discovery on Facebook.

Role
Lead Product Designer
Scope
0 to 1, concept to launch
Timeline
2025-2026

01 — Overview

I led design for VAC, an ad discovery experience on Facebook that unlocked a new supply channel for Video Monetization.

From concept to launch, I turned organizational resistance into alignment while navigating competing product priorities, leading the team to deliver 3× its original performance target. VAC launched in Q4’25 to users in the US & Canada, reaching a $109M incremental revenue run rate for Meta.

In 2026, I evolved Ad Chaining from a standalone project into an established product area, with 8+ engineers across multiple workstreams focused on expanding supply activation.

02 — Problem

Discovery kept flowing.
Ads hit a wall

With the introduction of Reels on Facebook, people could seamlessly move from one video to the next, exploring content based on their interests. A video click in Feed became an entry point to continuous video discovery.

While video discovery evolved, the ad experience did not. Clicking an ad still took people to a single destination, where they either converted or backed out to Feed.

03 — Insight

Clicking an ad is a signal of commercial intent

People who click an ad in Feed show stronger interest and are more likely to convert than those who passively scroll past. This behavior is particularly pronounced among Power Users, who have clicked 6+ Feed ads in the past 28 days, indicating a willingness to explore products and businesses.

What if an ad click could lead to more than a conversion?

What if it became the beginning of commercial discovery?

04 — Opportunity

Turn moments of commercial intent into continued ad discovery

Create a seamless way for people to explore products and businesses of interest, extending their ad journey beyond a single conversion.

05 — Current State

Production ad experiences can be grouped into 2 categories based on advertiser objective

  1. Engage audiences through Upper & Mid Funnel ObjectivesBrand Awareness, Video Views, Post Engagement, Page Likes, Link Clicks
  2. Drive conversions through Lower Funnel ObjectivesOnsite Conversions, Lead Generation, Landing Page Views, Offsite Conversions, App Installs

Ads with Upper & Mid Funnel Objectives

Clicking an ad video in Feed opened a fullscreen video viewer with a path to the brand’s profile page.

Ads with Lower Funnel Objectives

Clicking an ad video in Feed opened a split screen, with the video persisting at the top and an ad destination displayed below. The destination could be a webpage, an app store page, a lead generation form, a Messenger prompt, and more.

A new ad discovery experience must support these objectives and maintain their destinations, attribution, and performance

06 — Solution

VAC: An ad discovery experience that turns a single ad click into a continuous stream of relevant video ads

Video Ad Chaining (VAC)

Turns one ad click in Feed into a continuous stream of relevant video ads.

07 — Conviction

Design was only half the equation. The other half was building conviction to move it forward

08 — Results

3× Target

Delivered 3 times the product team's original ad performance target.

$109M iRev

Reached $109M incremental revenue run rate for Meta in 2025.

09 — Beyond Launch

Growing Ad Chaining into a product area

VAC's results proved the opportunity was bigger than the original scope. I drove that expansion, proposing new directions for ad discovery that grew Ad Chaining from a standalone project into a fully staffed product area in 2026, focused on expanding supply activation across new surfaces and formats.

Multi-Ad Chaining (MAC)

Lets people pick from a grid of ads, each opening its own chain in Reels.

Similar Ad Pivot (SAP)

Lets people pivot from a Reels ad to similar ads in a single tap.

Commerce Chaining (CC)

Blends recommended ads with content people love, turning shopping into discovery.
