MAC · Multi-Ad Chaining

Interactive prototype · a grid of ads, each the seed of a Reels ad chain · the FMSS IAB sheet and edge swipe back, shared

A Suggested for you grid of ads. Tap the Oakley ad: it grows into Reels and seeds a chain of related ads; swipe up through it (Oakley → LensCrafters → Ray-Ban). Learn more on the Oakley ad opens oakley.com in the in-app browser full screen; ×, Escape or a swipe down returns to the reel. Back (or an edge swipe from the left) returns to the grid.
