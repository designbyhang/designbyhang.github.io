VAC · Feed → Split Screen → Reels

Interactive prototype · the FMSS player controls and IAB sheet, shared

Tap the video ad in Feed to open Split Screen: the video plays on top with its live controls (timestamp and scrubber, from FMSS) and porsche.com loads in the in-app browser (IAB) sheet below. The controls fade out, leaving only ×; drag the scrubber to seek while they show. Tap the video, or swipe the sheet or the video down, to go into Reels, then swipe up through the chain. Back (or × in Split Screen) returns to Feed. The ad's call to action (the porsche.com footer in Feed, Learn more in Reels) opens porsche.com in the in-app browser full screen; ×, Escape or a swipe down returns to where you were.
