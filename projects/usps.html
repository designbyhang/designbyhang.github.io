01 — Overview

USPS was ready to unveil its latest innovations to America. What it didn't need was another dense, overly educational product demonstration. With full creative control over the project, I took the opportunity to experiment, building a bold, colorful visual language through illustration and animation that turned dry product information into something digestible, memorable, and fun to explore.
