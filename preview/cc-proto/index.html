CC · Commerce Chaining

Interactive prototype · "Explore more like this" on a Reels ad opens a category row of ads and creator posts · swipe up within a category, left and right across them

A Nike reel in Feed; swipe up to a Nespresso ad and tap "Explore more like this" in the bar at the bottom. Commerce Chaining opens on Coffee: swipe up through Blue Bottle, Starbucks and La Colombe, or swipe left (or tap a tab) across Home, Movie, Fashion and Beauty, where ads sit beside creator posts with their commerce cards. Universal's "View Storefront" opens its storefront; × closes it. The chevron (or Escape) returns to the Nespresso reel. Tap a reel to pause it; the comment icon on Nespresso opens its comments.
