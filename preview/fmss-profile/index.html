Profile → In-App Browser

FMSS · Nike reel → brand profile → peek sheet / floating card → in-app browser (IAB), rebuilt from the “Profile & Browse” recording

Tap Nike on the reel to open its profile. A peek of nike.com slides up; scroll the profile and the peek collapses into a floating card (scrolling back up keeps the card until you reach the very top, where the peek springs back). Tap the peek or the card to expand the full-screen in-app browser (IAB); × (or Escape, or a pull down at the page top) closes it back to the card. The card’s × dismisses only the card. Back, or a swipe from the left edge, returns to the reel.
