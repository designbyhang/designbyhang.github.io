  7. I design at the intersection of products, systems, and strategy. Over the past 10 years, I’ve built experiences used by billions of people, taken products from 0→1, and turned ambiguous problems into clear directions that teams can rally around. At Meta, much of my work has involved navigating the messy space between user needs, business goals, technical constraints, and organizational complexity, and finding a way through. I’m at my best when the path isn't obvious: framing the problem, seeing the opportunity others might miss, and turning a strong point of view into something real, useful, and scalable.
