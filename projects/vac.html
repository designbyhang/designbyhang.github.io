01 — Overview

I led design for VAC, an ad discovery experience on Facebook that unlocked a new supply channel for Video Monetization.

From concept to launch, I turned organizational resistance into alignment while navigating competing product priorities, leading the team to deliver 3× its original performance target. VAC launched in Q4’25 to users in the US & Canada, reaching a $109M incremental revenue run rate for Meta.

In 2026, I evolved Ad Chaining from a standalone project into an established product area, with 8+ engineers across multiple workstreams focused on expanding supply activation.

02 — Problem

With the introduction of Reels on Facebook, people could seamlessly move from one video to the next, exploring content based on their interests.

A video click in Feed became an entry point to continuous video discovery.

While video discovery evolved, the ad experience did not. Clicking an ad still took people to a single destination, where they either converted or backed out to Feed.

What if an ad click could lead to more than a conversion?

What if it became the beginning of commercial discovery?

03 — Key Findings

Clicking an ad is a signal of commercial intent

People who click an ad in Feed show stronger interest and are more likely to convert than those who passively scroll past. This behavior is particularly pronounced among Power Users, who have clicked 6+ Feed ads in the past 28 days, indicating a willingness to explore products and businesses.

04 — Opportunity

Turn moments of commercial intent into continued ad discovery

Create a seamless way for people to explore products and businesses of interest, extending their ad journey beyond a single conversion.

Production ad experiences can be grouped into 2 categories based on advertiser objective

  1. Engage audiences through Upper & Mid Funnel ObjectivesBrand Awareness, Video Views, Post Engagement, Page Likes, Link Clicks
  2. Drive conversions through Lower Funnel ObjectivesOnsite Conversions, Lead Generation, Landing Page Views, Offsite Conversions, App Installs

Ads with Upper & Mid Funnel Objectives

Clicking an ad video in Feed opened a fullscreen video viewer with a path to the brand’s profile page.

Ads with Lower Funnel Objectives

Clicking an ad video in Feed opened a split screen, with the video persisting at the top and an ad destination displayed below. The destination could be a webpage, an app store page, a lead generation form, a Messenger prompt, and more.

A new ad discovery experience must support these objectives and maintain their destinations, attribution, and performance

06 — How I Build Conviction

Challenging the Status Quo

VAC broke a pattern that had defined Facebook ads since Feed existed: an ad click ends in conversion or a return to Feed. Reworking that model raised real concerns at the Director and VP level, including from the Head of Design, about downstream effects on the Feed experience and fit with the broader App Simplification strategy.

In those reviews, I anchored the conversation in the commercial intent signal that had shaped the opportunity, shifting the discussion from Why touch a working model to What was being left on the table.

To move from data to buy in, I partnered with Engineering to build the experience at near production quality, held to the same bar as the original prototype's promise. Leads dogfooded it directly, and their green light moved VAC from a contested idea to a backed proposal.

Protecting Ecosystem Health

Ads and organic video compete for the same user attention. Growing ad engagement at the cost of organic consumption would put VAC in direct conflict with the metrics other teams were built around.

To settle whether that tradeoff was worth taking, I proposed a validation approach combining sustained testing with direct user feedback, tracking VAC performance across H1 and H2 2025 alongside in-app sentiment surveys. The results, User Value Analysis and UXR reports showing positive user value with no cost to organic consumption or app engagement, removed the ecosystem risk as a blocker to rollout.

Mitigating Revenue Cannibalization

Feed is Facebook’s most monetization-efficient surface, far exceeding immersive formats like Reels and Stories.

VAC uses the Reels Player to chain ads. While this increases ad density, shifting traffic from Feed to Reels could create a risk of app-level revenue loss.

I worked with PM and DS to establish the guardrails and exit criteria, then aligned with Feed’s ad partners on the experiment plan.

We tested different ad loads, scroll depths, and user cohorts, identifying a candidate that delivered app-level revenue gains while keeping Feed revenue cannibalization within an acceptable threshold.
