Case Study · Citi · Mobile

Citi Mobile

01 — Overview

In 2017, I was brought in to define Citi’s mobile visual language, establishing a modular tile architecture and color theming across three customer segments. I led the development of its first platform-agnostic mobile design system, scaling it across the FinTech and Core design teams.

Working closely with Product and Research, we co-created new banking experiences with customers, bringing features including Account Aggregation and Spending Insights from testing through launch, reaching 18M+ active mobile users.
